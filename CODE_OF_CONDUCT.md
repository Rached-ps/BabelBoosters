# Code of conduct

This is a project where people do things for the first time. Nearly everyone
here is a beginner at something, including the people who are not beginners at
this.

That is the whole reason this document exists, and it is short on purpose.

## What we expect

**Be kind, and assume the other person is doing their best.** Someone who does
not know what a pull request is has not failed at anything. They just have not
met the word yet, the same way you had not a while ago.

**Answer questions properly.** "Just google it" is not an answer here. Neither
is answering with three more words the person does not know.

**Nobody's language, script or spelling is wrong.** Writing your language in
Latin letters is not a lesser version of writing it in its own script.
A regional form is not a mistake. An uncertain translation with an honest note
is worth more than a confident wrong one.

**Say when you are not sure.** It helps everyone, and it makes it safe for the
next person to say it too.

**Take feedback on your work as being about your work.** If a maintainer
suggests a change, that is how open source works for everyone, at every level of
experience.

## What is not acceptable

- Insults, mockery, or personal attacks — including in comments on someone's
  pull request.
- Making someone feel stupid for not knowing something.
- Harassment of any kind: sexual attention or advances, slurs, or comments
  targeting someone's caste, religion, region, language, gender, sexuality,
  disability, appearance or background.
- Sharing someone's private information — their address, their phone number,
  their private messages — without their clear permission.
- Deliberately derailing, spamming or vandalising the project.
- Editing or deleting someone else's file. Every contributor's file is theirs.
  If you think there is a mistake in one, open an issue and say so politely.

## Where this applies

Anywhere this project happens: pull requests, issues, comments, reviews, the
club's chat groups and workshops, and any event where you are here as part of
Accelerate.

## If something goes wrong

Tell a maintainer or an Accelerate organiser. You can:

- Speak to any organiser in person at a club session, or
- Email **<add the club's contact address here>**

You will be taken seriously and you will not be blamed for reporting. Your
report will be kept private, shared only with the people who need it to sort the
problem out. You can report something that happened to you, or something you saw
happen to someone else.

If the person you need to report is a maintainer, tell a different maintainer or
an organiser — you never have to report someone to themselves.

## What we will do

We will listen to everyone involved before deciding anything.

Depending on what happened, that might mean a quiet word, a public correction, a
request for an apology, having a comment or pull request removed, or being asked
to leave the project and club spaces. We will explain what we decided to the
person affected.

Most things that go wrong here will be someone being careless rather than cruel,
and a quiet word will fix it. We would rather hear about something small early
than something large late.
